## Q8 罗盘回顾—判断对错

最后一组8题，覆盖肯定与否定呼应、助动词匹配、名词/代词倒装、Never强调及定语从句基础。答错先订正，再播放正确句。

| 题号 | 题干与选项/词块 | 正确答案（含接受形式） | 干扰项或常见误答 | 答错时中文解释 | 答对后音频与正确句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q8.1 | Leo: I like stories. Grandpa回应“我也是”：So do I. | 对 | 错 | 前句一般现在时实义动词，回应I用do。 | `audio/ex_q8_1.mp3`：So do I. |
| Q8.2 | Mia: I can't wait. Lily回应：Neither can't I. | 错 | 对 | neither已含否定，应为Neither can I；这里意思是也等不及。 | `audio/ex_q8_2.mp3`：Neither can I. |
| Q8.3 | Sam: I visited London. Leo回应：So did I. | 对 | 错 | 过去时呼应选did。 | `audio/ex_q8_3.mp3`：So did I. |
| Q8.4 | Sam: I have packed. Lily说Leo也一样：So have Leo. | 错 | 对 | Leo三单，应为has。 | `audio/ex_q8_4.mp3`：So has Leo. |
| Q8.5 | Here comes Biscuit. Here he comes. | 对 | 错 | 名词Biscuit可倒装；人称代词he不这样倒装。 | `audio/ex_q8_5.mp3`：Here comes Biscuit. Here he comes. |
| Q8.6 | Never I have seen the compass so bright. | 错 | 对 | Never前置时，have在I前。 | `audio/ex_q8_6.mp3`：Never have I seen the compass so bright. |
| Q8.7 | This is the compass which brought us home. | 对 | 错 | which可指物并在从句中作主语；that也可。 | `audio/ex_q8_7.mp3`：This is the compass which brought us home. |
| Q8.8 | The letter which Grandpa left it is here. | 错 | 对 | which已是从句宾语，it重复，要删掉。 | `audio/ex_q8_8.mp3`：The letter which Grandpa left is here. |
